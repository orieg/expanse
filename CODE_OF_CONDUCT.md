# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. The full text is authoritative; what follows is the
short form of what it asks and how it is applied here.

Participants are expected to be respectful, to accept technical criticism
without taking it personally, and to keep discussion on the work. Harassment,
personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing others' private
information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Technical disagreement is welcome and expected. This repository holds its claims
to an evidence standard (`AGENTS.md` §8) — saying a number is unsupported, a
benchmark is unfair, or a design is wrong is on-topic conduct, not a breach of
it. How it is said still matters.

## Scope

Applies in this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, code, and
commit messages, and to anyone representing the project in a public space.

## Enforcement

Report a concern to <nicolas@brousse.info>, or privately through GitHub's
**Security → Report a vulnerability** form if you would rather it not sit in an
inbox. The project has a single maintainer, so a report goes to that maintainer
and stays confidential.

Reports are acknowledged and acted on as they arrive rather than against a
published schedule. Responses range from a correction, to editing or removing
content, to blocking an account, in proportion to the behaviour.
